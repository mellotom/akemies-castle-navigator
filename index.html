dual vco · four-operator fm from a real yamaha opl3 chip · six algorithms · eight waves · up to five-voice chords
01

what it is

ac_01
dual vco
two oscillators, A and B, each with its own v/oct, tuning and output
the engine
a new-old-stock yamaha ymf262 (opl3) chip · 4 operators, 6 algorithms, 8 waves
sounds like
yamaha dx100 · dx21 · tx81z territory
the catch
a real chip: some steppiness, small clicks, aliasing when pushed
02

algorithms

ac_02 · the blue button · fig.01
[ alg ]
press to step through the 6 · an LED marks the one in use
first two
two separate 2-operator vcos · one pair fm’d (1 → 2), the other mixed (3 + 4) · the second swaps them
last four
one 4-operator voice on both A and B · same tone, separate pitch, feedback and output
rule of thumb
more operators and more → arrows = a more complex sound
chords
oscillator A can play chords in every algorithm
10

ac_10 · from sine to fm
1

03

operators 1 – 4

ac_03 · fig.02
wave
8 shapes · sine, half-sine, abs sine, pulse sine, even sine, camel sine, square, log saw
mult
multiplier 1 – 15 × the v/oct pitch
level
0 – 63 · how loud that operator is
modulator
an operator that feeds an arrow · its level = brightness, not volume
carrier
the last in a chain or a mixed one · its level = volume
__fig.02 : modulator vs carrieralgorithm 1, osc A
04

the knobs explained

ac_04 · white · grey · small
white knob
the setting itself · cv is added on top of it
small knob
below each white knob · cv amount (−|+ = attenuverter, | = attenuator)
grey wave knob
nothing patched: picks the wave · cv patched: becomes the cv amount
no amount
v/oct fb (osc B) inv · straight in
cv range
0 – 5 V · chord and v/oct take about 0 – 10 V
05

feedback · chords

ac_05 · osc A section
fb feedback
0 – 7 · the first operator in each chain modulates itself · coarser, noisier
osc B
uses A’s feedback until something is patched into B’s fb jack
ch chord
16 chords on osc A: 5th, octaves ×3, maj, min, sus, aug, dim, dom7, maj7, min7, 9th, detuned ×3
inv
more voltage = more inversion · an lfo here plays arpeggio-like melodies
louder!
chords add voices · turn the last operator levels down
06

why does it do that?

ac_06 · the chip’s character
clicks
sweeping a level clicks, most in its top quarter
never silent
a level near zero can still bleed through
out of tune
feedback shifts tuning · non-sine waves add inharmonics
slow modulation
cv works up to low audio rates only
noise wall
heavy feedback + high levels can alias and distort · back off
08

four patches

ac_08 · manual + fm basics
pure sinealg 1 or 2
wave, multiplier, feedback, chord fully left · only the carrier level up · tune and calibrate here
chord melodiesany + chord
pick a chord, a slow lfo into inv for pseudo-sequenced lines
fm drums4-op · chord max
snappy envelope into osc A v/oct and a vca after A out · mult it to other inputs · ride feedback
brighten with an envelopeserial alg
envelope into a modulator’s level cv · the attack gets bright, then mellows
